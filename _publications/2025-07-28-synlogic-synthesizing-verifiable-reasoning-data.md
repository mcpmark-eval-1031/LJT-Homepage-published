SynLogic introduces a scalable framework for synthesizing verifiable reasoning data, enabling large language models to learn logical reasoning and related skills without relying solely on human-authored data. By automatically generating questions with ground-truth answers that can be automatically verified, the approach facilitates large-scale training and reinforcement-learning-based improvement of reasoning capabilities and generalizes beyond classical logical reasoning.
