This paper studies the perception bottleneck that limits vision-language models (VLMs) from understanding charts. Through fine-grained analysis, we show that much of the chart-understanding failure stems from limitations in perceiving and extracting visual information from charts rather than from reasoning deficits, highlighting a key bottleneck for improving VLM performance on visual analytics. Code repository: Vision4Chart.
