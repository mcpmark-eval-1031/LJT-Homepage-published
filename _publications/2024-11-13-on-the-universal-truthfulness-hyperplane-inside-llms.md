This work examines whether a universal linear direction—referred to as a "truthfulness hyperplane"—exists inside large language models that separates factually correct statements from incorrect ones. By probing the internal representations of LLMs, we find evidence that truthfulness is encoded along a robust linear subspace, shedding light on how models track factual knowledge and opening avenues for interpretability-driven interventions. Code repository: Universal_Truthfulness_Hyperplane.
